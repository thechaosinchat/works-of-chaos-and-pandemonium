---
title: "A Gift in Disguise"
created: 2026-06-03
edited: 2026-08-05
---

<div class="poem centered">
  <p>The fathers of video games must be proud of themselves. For creating a work able to<br />
  immerse, charm, intoxicate; break, sway, and ruin fates;<br />
  All for a moment of enjoyment.</p>
  <p>Solace.<br />
  Escape.<br />
  Freedom.<br />
  A safe haven against boredom.</p>
  <p>Far away from uncertainties.</p>
  <p>Somewhere with wonder. Excitement.</p>
  <p>With joy. Amazement.</p>
  <p>An empty apartment of no regulations.</p>
  <p>Spacious compartments of absolute contentment.</p>
</div>

Yet that’s not what it is. Or what it’ll ever be.

Everyone already knows: the neighbors' sleep you disrupted, that scrawled red D-minus you received, those disappointed looks teachers and fellow students you endured, the sighs of anguish you make as you trudge your way back into your dark dorm room again, all due to that evil. Those feelings of junkie-joy and self-loathing as you arc your back, fingers trembling, aching to caress those controls, the routine snacking with your dorito’s contaminated digits, chomping and chewing obnoxiously, all stemming from this invention you chose to indulge.

And it’s not like you are numb to these facts at all. The moment you lie on your bed, eyes bloodshot with an inexplicable dryness in your mouth, your heart arriving at the gates of tachycardia, and an unstoppable screen of imagination within your eyelids, you instantly drown in a tsunami of fear closely followed by crashing waves of resignation. Friendly chats turn into lifeless nods; others with free will, thoughts, and emotions turn into characters of programmed moves, dialogue, lore. The dividing line blurs until it fades away.

So I implore you to pause. Imagine a life without this 8th deadly sin. The awful sin created by the fathers in hopes of lifting humanity. This horrendous drug.

<div class="poem centered"><p>A drug no one can control.</p></div>
