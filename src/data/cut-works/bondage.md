<div class="poem">
  <p>I turned, walked, and laid down,<br />
  Face up, palms down, legs sprawled out<br />
  upon the moist grass<br />
  After a crying of the morning clouds.</p>

  <p>My headphones blast noise.<br />
  My fingers wish to change it, my head wishes to peer around,<br />
  Yet Mother Earth’s green limbs pull me back,<br />
  Preventing my wishes, my body<br />
  A sacrifice to the heavens.</p>

  <p>Blades of grass sprinkle tears<br />
  My eyes follow suit, but nothing seeps out.<br />
  The night sky twinkles with eyes, forever watching<br />
  A million juries staring me down.</p>

  <p>My throat croaks out off-tuned lyrics,<br />
  My ears enveloped in viridescent fingers,<br />
  And yet minute minutes feel like eternity.<br />
  The sky, earth, and I, all in ties.</p>
</div>
