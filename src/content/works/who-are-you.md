title: "Who Are You?"
created: 2026-06-06
edited: 2026-06-22
---

A simple question with an innocent motive.

And I would answer with a syllable. And nobody would think twice.

Is this really who I am? A single syllable?

I want to be more. Two words, a first and a last. Three words with a prefix. A suffix too.

I want to be more. An intelligent monkey with two standing legs and ten nimble fingers. A human with experiences. Tons and tons of experiences and even more adventures.

I want to be more.

Visit Tokyo. Finish *Ultrakill*. Love myself. Start a protest. Fly a plane. Feel the pain of a loved one’s death. Watch *The Terminator* and other old school films. Get in a bar fight and come out victorious. Become a grandparent whose grandchild visits once a year to eat and leave.

I want to do more.

Play every video game. Read every book. Maneuver every vehicle of every type, from the Princess Andromeda to a Boeing 747. Become the ruler of every person. Learn every subject in the world. Think every possible thought, no matter how grandiose or controversial. Exist as every person who has ever existed, through the lens of their opinion and in the shoes that they walk in.

<div class="poem centered"><p>…</p><p>I can’t stop there.</p></div>

I need to do more.

How else am I going to find who I am?

<div class="poem vertical"><p>But</p><p>nothing</p><p>will</p><p>ever</p><p>be</p><p>enough.</p></div>

Get out of your comfort zone. Try something new!

There simply isn’t enough time to do anything. We will all turn to the quintessence of dust someday. It’s inevitable, yet life simply doesn’t allow you to do and experience everything. And that’s the beauty of it. But then… Who are you?
