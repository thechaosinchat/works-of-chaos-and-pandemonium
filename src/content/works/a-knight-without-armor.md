“I love youu, good night my baby” he says, lying on his bed, a foolish smile plastered over his face. “Night, love you” a sweet voice replies through the phone. The phone, having suffered through numerous all-night calls, displays a cute wallpaper of the two of them before their school’s homecoming, her head on his chest and his hand on her shoulder. He hangs up, his heart still racing. He turns and giggles, ecstatically imagining their future together after high school. He wants to have two kids, a boy and a girl, with a house in the suburbs, and to come home with her beautiful smile greeting him every time. He falls asleep eventually, dreaming of a romantic night walk with her, his hand relentlessly holding onto hers, never letting go. He wishes he could dream forever. In an instant, he’s jolted awake from the alarm on his phone. He turns it off and checks his phone. 7:35am. He was about to fall back asleep until he noticed he got a text. L: “can we talk?” He loses all drowsiness in an instant, mind swirling with possibilities. Did I do something wrong, did I hurt her, am I not enough for her? He imagines all sorts of discussions, almost falling over while fumbling through his morning routine. I love her, and she loves me, right?

Later in school, he runs into her. He tried to turn away, but she stopped him, pulling him aside from the crowd of people. She asks him if they could take a break. She says she wants to have some fun in high school, flirting and not being confined to a boyfriend of 9 months. She knew it’s an outrageous request, she knew she still loved him, but she still wanted to try. She’s surprised when he says he will do anything to support her, to let her enjoy herself. Her heart breaks when he says he wants her to have fun, even if it costs him his heart. “I love you”. He repeats it like a mantra, pushing back the tears and taping up the cuts in his heart. Despite everything, he still attempts to comfort her, but his weak smile simply tears her apart even more.

Right as he returns to his room, he collapses on his bed, unable to stop the tears anymore. He cries and cries, sheets soaked in tears. He cries and cries like a newborn baby. He cries and cries, his heart overflowing with every sacrifice for her. He lost his friends, deleted Instagram, spent countless nights talking, even biked through the frigid weather down 303, cars honking at him relentlessly for his stupidity, hands frozen and numb. But he had to. Had to do everything he’s done. Because he loved her. That’s the reason. Love. He would sacrifice every fiber of his soul for her because he loved her. That has to be the reason. But he knew that his mind was simply trying to cope. He suddenly stopped crying. Looked up at the ceiling blankly. He knew his own fate. He was destined to be an altruist: to raise people up while pushing himself down, to endure suffering and pain to prevent it upon others, to shoulder the burden alone. He knew: To be altruistic is to be.
