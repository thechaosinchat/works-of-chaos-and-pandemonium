---
title: "A Man without Company"
created: 2026-04-22
edited: 2026-07-05
---

When a man surrounds themselves in company, they cackle jovially to devious as well as light-hearted conversations. They surround themselves in speech and actions, hopping about with every step, colors emblazoned on their every move. They release their tension when surrounded in company, tentatively touching shoulders in times of hardships and talking tech-savvy terms used in computer games on a regular basis. When they are in a group with others, they join them and become just another one of the masses, excitedly chatting and gossiping about novel movies and party games. But, when he is on occasion separated from his company, his charm infects the nearby denizens, empowering the locals with the power of his cheerful and upbeat aura. He brings joy to everyone near him, a smile emerging on the faces of all.

And yet, when their company abandons him, and when their friends,

their neighbors, even their family abandons them,

they abandon life itself.

They find themselves alone in the cruel world.

They find themselves as just another person.

<div class="poem centered">
  <p>A nobody.</p>
  <p>There’s nobody to talk to.<br />There’s no one to come home to,<br />no home to return to.</p>
  <p>There’s no feelings to talk through,<br />no colors reflected in the eyes.</p>
  <p>There’s no neck raised above the horizon line,<br />no energy in the rise of fingers.<br />No hip and hop in every, begrudging, slow and wading step,<br />no unicorns and rainbows at the dead of night, only falling.</p>
  <p>Falling…</p>
  <p>Falling.</p>
</div>

Falling and clanking as they hit the bottom of a bottle. A bottle which contains their entire existence. An inescapable bottle.

<div class="poem centered"><p>And <span class="stretched">they</span> rest there until timejuststops.</p></div>
